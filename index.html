How to use Reselling HQ

Five minutes a week once it's set up.

Set up (one time, about 15 minutes)
  1. In your Google Sheet, choose File, Share, Publish to web. Pick the Inventory tab and Comma-separated values (.csv), then Publish and copy the link.
  2. Do the same for the Expenses tab.
  3. Open Settings in this app, enter your app password and paste both links. Check your stale limits, offer rules and budget, then Save.
  4. Press Run my weekly check.
Every week
  1. Make sure your sales and new stock are in the tracker sheet.
  2. Open this app and press the yellow button.
  3. Work through "This week's plan" and the stale stock list.
  4. Fix any rows listed under "Records and tax check".
Writing a listing

Go to Listings, add photos, type a line about the item (brand, model, size, condition, flaws), and press Write listing. Copy each section into eBay or Vinted. Always check sizes and condition before posting.

Replying to a buyer

Go to Replies, paste their message, add the listed price and your cost, then press Draft reply. It accepts offers above your lowest price (set in Settings) and counters ones below it.

It says my password is wrong

Open Settings and type the password again. It must match the APP_PASSWORD you set in Vercel.

It can't read my sheet

Check both links in Settings start with https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/ and end in output=csv. Or press "Use CSV files instead": open each tab, choose File, Download, CSV, then pick both files.

Are the drops and niche ideas live?

Yes. Claude searches the web during your weekly check for release dates and recent sold prices. Still double-check before you spend money. Your profit, stock and stale numbers come straight from your own sheet.

Install it on your phone

iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

Is my sheet private?

A sheet published to the web can be opened by anyone who has the exact link, though it isn't listed or searchable. Don't share the links, and you can unpublish any time from File, Share, Publish to web.
