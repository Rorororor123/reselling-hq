How to use Reselling HQ

Five minutes a week once it's set up.

Set up (one time, about 10 minutes)
  1. Open Settings. Under Bring in your old tracker, choose your old spreadsheet (or skip this and start fresh).
  2. Check your stale limits, offer rules, budget and monthly goal in Settings, then Save.
  3. Add new things as they happen: orders when you buy, sales when you sell, expenses when you pay. The + button on a phone does all four.
  4. Go to Weekly check and press Run my weekly check.
Orders and the dashboard

Add each thing you buy on the Orders screen (the + button on a phone). Move it along as it ships, and when it's delivered tap Move into stock. The dashboard shows your profit, what's on the way, your stock, pre-orders and anything that needs doing. Tap Customise to choose which boxes show.

Every week
  1. Make sure this week's sales, new stock and expenses are in the app.
  2. Open this app, go to Weekly check and press Run my weekly check.
  3. Work through "This week's plan" and the stale stock list.
  4. Fix anything listed under "Records and tax check" or "Needs action".
  5. Download a backup (Settings, Download backup) and keep it somewhere safe.
Writing a listing

Go to Listings, add photos, type a line about the item (brand, model, size, condition, flaws), and press Write listing. Copy each section into eBay or Vinted. Always check sizes and condition before posting.

Replying to a buyer

Go to Replies, paste their message, add the listed price and your cost, then press Draft reply. It accepts offers above your lowest price (set in Settings) and counters ones below it.

Signing in on a new phone

Open the app, type your email and tap Email me a sign-in link. Open the link on that same phone, or type the code from the email into the app (handy if you installed the app on your home screen). You stay signed in until you press Sign out in Settings. To skip the email, set a password in Settings.

Bringing in my old spreadsheet

In Settings, choose Bring in your old tracker and pick the Excel file. From Google Sheets, choose File, Download, Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) first. It reads tabs with an "Item Name" column (stock and sales) and an "Amount" column (expenses), shows you what it found, and skips anything already in the app, so it's safe to do twice.

Are the drops and niche ideas live?

Yes. Claude searches the web during your weekly check for release dates and recent sold prices. Still double-check before you spend money. Your profit, stock and stale numbers come straight from what you've entered in the app.

Install it on your phone

iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

Is my data private?

Your stock, sales, orders and expenses are saved in your account and only you can see them. If you published your old Google Sheet to the web for the earlier version of this app, you can now unpublish it: in the sheet, choose File, Share, Publish to web, then Stop publishing.
